Data Structures & Algorithms

Twenty-one chapters on the small number of ideas that underpin every program that has to be fast. Complexity analysis you can actually apply, the structures and what they cost, the algorithms and why they work, and the dozen patterns that solve most interview and real-world problems. Every structure and algorithm here runs live in the page — step through a rotation, watch a heap sift, race two search strategies. The unit is complete: all twenty-one chapters, 42 interactive demos, and a closing reference with a searchable glossary and a 42-question self-quiz.

Learning it for the first time

In order. Chapters 2 and 7 are the ones that unlock everything else, and both reward slow reading.

  1. Chapters 1 – 6 — cost, then the linear structures
  2. Chapter 7 — recursion, properly
  3. Chapters 8 – 13 — sorting and trees
  4. Chapters 14 – 21 — graphs, then patterns

Preparing for interviews

The pattern chapters carry most of the weight, but they only work once the structures underneath are automatic.

  1. Chapter 2 — you will be asked to justify every complexity
  2. Chapters 6, 9, 13 — hashing, binary search, heaps
  3. Chapters 19 – 20 — patterns and DP
  4. Chapter 21 — the pattern-to-problem map

How a chapter is built

Watch it run

Structures and algorithms animate step by step in the page — the real algorithm, not a recorded animation.

Cost, always

Every operation gets its time and space complexity, with the reasoning rather than a memorised table.

When to use what

Decision tables comparing structures on the operations you actually perform, including the constant factors.

Implementation

Clean Python for each structure and algorithm, with the edge cases that break naive versions marked.
