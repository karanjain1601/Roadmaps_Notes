Data Analyst

The job is not running queries. It is turning a vague question into one the data can settle, arriving at an answer that survives scrutiny, and saying it in a way that changes a decision. The tooling is the easy part, and it is where most material about this role stops.

What this unit is

This is the reasoning unit. Chapter 6 covers choosing between Excel, SQL and Python and where each hits its ceiling, and that is the extent of the tooling — because the Python for Data Analysis unit covers pandas and the plotting stack properly, SQL covers the query language end to end, and BI Analyst covers the platform and dashboard side. Duplicating them here would produce three shallow units instead of one deep one.

What is left is the part that transfers between tools and does not go out of date: knowing what a number means before you compute with it, cleaning without quietly changing the answer, telling the difference between a real effect and noise, and recognising the handful of errors that account for most wrong analyses. Most of those errors are not statistical mistakes. They are the wrong question, an unnoticed change in what a column measures, a join that duplicated rows, or a comparison between two groups that were never comparable.

No prerequisites. Chapters 1–3 are about the job and the data; 4–6 about getting to something you can analyse; 7–9 about describing it; 10–13 about inference, which is where the expensive mistakes live; and 14–18 about the analyses that get asked for repeatedly and how to deliver a finding that lands.

Two habits the unit argues for

Write down what would change the decision

Before querying anything. If the answer is "nothing", the analysis is decoration and it is better to find that out in the first conversation than after three days. If it is something specific, that sentence tells you which number you need, how precise it has to be, and when you can stop.

Say what you did to the data

Every analysis involves choices — which rows to exclude, how to handle nulls, which window to compare against. Each one moves the answer. A finding that reports the number without the choices is unauditable, and the choices are usually where a disagreement actually lives.
