Data Engineer

Data engineering is the work of making data usable by someone else, reliably, at a volume and freshness they can depend on. This unit covers the whole lifecycle — from systems you do not control, through storage and processing, to the dashboards and models that consume the result — and the operational discipline that keeps it running once real people rely on it.

WHAT THIS ASSUMES

Comfort reading SQL and Python. You do not need distributed-systems experience — chapter 15 builds what you need. Where a chapter leans on a neighbouring unit it says so and links across.

WHAT IT DELIBERATELY OMITS

Vendor click-paths and console screenshots, which go stale within months. The unit teaches the model behind each tool so you can read its documentation quickly rather than memorising a menu.

HOW THE DEMOS WORK

Each one runs the real algorithm in the page — a shuffle, a watermark, a compatibility check — with the inputs exposed. They are built to be broken on purpose; that is usually where the lesson is.
