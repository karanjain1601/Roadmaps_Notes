Machine Learning

The complete ML engineer roadmap, written out as study notes you can actually learn from. Twenty-four chapters covering the maths you genuinely use, how raw data becomes features, every major algorithm with its assumptions and failure modes, honest evaluation, deep learning from the perceptron to the transformer, and what changes the day a model leaves your laptop. Interactive demos throughout — the point is to move a slider and watch the idea behave.

All twenty-four chapters are written. Every chapter is one page in this folder. Pages link to each other but depend on nothing outside it, so you can copy the folder to a USB stick, open any page offline and it still works — diagrams, demos and all.

If you are starting from scratch

Read in order. The maths chapters are written for someone who last saw a derivative at school, and every symbol is defined where it first appears.

  1. Chapters 1 – 6, slowly
  2. Chapters 7 – 15, with a dataset open
  3. Chapters 16 – 20 once classical ML feels boring
  4. Chapters 21 – 24 last

If you can already code and want the ML

Skim the maths for notation, then spend your time on the modelling chapters where the real decisions live.

  1. Chapter 10, then 11 – 15
  2. Back-fill maths from 2 – 5 when a formula bites
  3. Chapters 16 – 19
  4. Chapter 22 before you ship anything

If you are preparing for interviews

Interviews test the joins between topics: why this loss, why this metric, what breaks at scale.

  1. Chapter 10 — bias, variance, generalisation
  2. Chapter 15 — metrics and validation
  3. Chapters 11 – 13 — derive, do not memorise
  4. Chapter 16 — backpropagation by hand

How a chapter is built

Explain, then formalise

Plain-language intuition first, then the notation, then the code. If a formula appears, the sentence under it says what each symbol means.

Demos you can break

Sliders and canvases wired to the real algorithm. Turn the learning rate up until gradient descent explodes; that memory sticks.

Failure modes named

Every method gets a section on when it quietly gives wrong answers, because that is what separates working models from demos.

Self-checks

Questions at the end of each chapter with hidden answers. Attempt before revealing; recall beats rereading.
